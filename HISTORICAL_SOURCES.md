# Historical sources for the Loaísa–Elcano campaign

The historical campaign uses broad geographic mission legs for gameplay. Mission endpoints are not intended to claim that the expedition made an exact documented stop at every endpoint.

Key framing used by the game:

- The fleet of seven ships departed A Coruña on 24 July 1525 under García Jofre de Loaísa, with Juan Sebastián Elcano as second-in-command / pilot major.
- It sailed toward the Canary Islands, continued south in the Atlantic, crossed toward South America, and reached Patagonia before attempting the Strait of Magellan.
- The surviving ships entered the Pacific in May 1526 and were scattered by severe weather.
- Loaísa and Elcano died during the Pacific crossing before the surviving flagship reached the Moluccas.
- The Santa María de la Victoria continued through the western Pacific, reaching the Marshall Islands region, Guam, Mindanao and finally the Moluccas.

Useful references:

- Armada Española / Instituto de Historia y Cultura Naval — “Navegación Virtual García Jofre de Loaísa”.
- Charles E. Nowell, “The Loaisa Expedition and the Ownership of the Moluccas”, Pacific Historical Review (1936).
- Harry Kelsey, “Finding the Way Home: Spanish Exploration of the Round-Trip Route across the Pacific Ocean”, Western Historical Quarterly (1986).

The game deliberately labels modern wind/current data as plausible environmental conditions rather than literal reconstructed weather from 1525–1526.
